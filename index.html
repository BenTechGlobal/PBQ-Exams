Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

CodingHamme Practice Exam (“CodingHamme,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our practice-exam platform and related services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account & Registration Data

When you create an account, we may store a username, email address, and a password (or password hash) that you provide. These credentials are used solely to authenticate access to your practice products and saved progress.

1.2 Practice & Progress Data

To deliver exam history, bookmarks, notes, and saved sessions, we store practice-related data in your browser’s local storage (for example: question attempts, scores, bookmarks, and configuration preferences). This data remains on your device unless you clear it or use the in-app Reset Data feature.

1.3 Technical & Usage Data

Like most web applications, standard browser and device information (such as browser type, approximate region inferred from network, and basic error logs) may be processed to keep the service secure and functional. We do not sell this information.

1.4 Feedback Submissions

If you submit content or software feedback from within an exam session, the message you type, the related question identifier, product code, and a timestamp may be transmitted to our support channel so we can improve materials and resolve issues.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the practice-exam experience.
  • To authenticate users and restore saved exams, notes, and history on the same device.
  • To respond to support and feedback requests.
  • To protect against abuse, fraud, or security incidents.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

CodingHamme primarily relies on browser local storage (not third-party advertising cookies) to persist login state, product activations, exam progress, bookmarks, and notes on the device you use. Clearing site data in your browser or using a Reset Data action will remove this information from that device.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data only when you request it, when a service provider processes data on our behalf, when required by law, or when necessary to protect rights and safety.

5. Data Retention

Practice history and notes stored in local storage remain until you delete them, clear browser data, or perform a full data reset. Feedback submissions are retained only as long as needed to investigate and improve the product.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction/objection, portability, and CCPA-style rights. We do not sell personal information. Contact us via in-app feedback or the community channel to exercise these rights.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to a client-side practice platform. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; you are responsible for securing the device on which you practice.

8. Children’s Privacy

CodingHamme Practice Exam is intended for adult learners and professional certification candidates. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, use the in-app Send Feedback feature or the community channels linked from promotional cards.
